Bohemian Wind

From met mast data to a permitting pack: data checks, long-term correction, wind map, layout and energy, noise and shadow flicker, and the pack.

Developer estimates prepared by Bohemian Renewables with open-source tools. Nothing here is an accredited or bankable assessment, and it must not be used for financing decisions.

WHAT THIS SETUP DOES
It installs the program for you alone, without administrator rights, with its own copy of Python and of the open-source packages it needs. It changes nothing else on this computer, and you can remove it again under Settings, Apps.

YOUR DATA STAYS HERE
The program runs on this computer only and sends nothing anywhere. It downloads only what a project asks for: reference wind data (NASA POWER), terrain heights (SRTM) and, when you press "Get buildings", building outlines (OpenStreetMap and Microsoft). Projects are kept in Documents\Bohemian Wind unless you choose another folder; removing the program leaves them where they are.

LICENCES
Bohemian Wind is under the MIT licence, shown on the previous page. Python and the packages come under their own open-source licences. The CFD wind map runs OpenFOAM, free software under the GNU General Public License v3, as separate programs; they come with their licence and their complete source code in the folder "openfoam". All of them are in the folder "licenses" of the installation; PACKAGES.txt and LIBRARIES.txt there say what is inside and under which licence.
